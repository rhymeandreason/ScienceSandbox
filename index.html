Kodolab is a library of in-depth 3D interactives designed for teaching threshold concepts — the ideas a student has to cross before the rest of biology makes sense. Work in progress, check back soon.

Seeing it beats reading about it. A static diagram flattens the thing it explains, and the next textbook draws it differently. Students spend their attention reconciling pictures instead of learning the concept. These pages pull the scattered versions of an idea together into models you can turn in your hands.
