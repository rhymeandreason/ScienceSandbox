    Kodolab is a library of in-depth 3D interactives designed for teaching threshold concepts — the ideas a student has to cross before the rest of biology makes sense. Work in progress, check back soon.

    Seeing it beats reading about it. Students spend their attention reconciling static pictures instead of learning the concept. These pages pull the scattered versions of an idea together into models you can turn in your hands.
