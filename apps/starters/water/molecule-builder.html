Build a Molecule

Bonding bench

Atoms don't bond because you want them to — they bond when they have room. The yellow dots are valence electrons: a single dot is one that's free to share, a pair sitting together is already spoken for, and a pair between two atoms is a bond. Fill every open slot and you've made a molecule; try to add one more and it will refuse. Watch what happens when a metal meets a nonmetal.
