## What changed vs. the *Biology 2e* chart

**Two rows improved:**
- Free energy — correct bond terminology at §6.4 removes the seeded misconception
- Pathway dynamics — feedback inhibition moved to §6.5, *before* glycolysis rather than after

**One row got materially worse:**
- Steady state — losing the animal-physiology chapters eliminated the only section that named it

**Two unchanged:** physical basis, macromolecular thermodynamics.

The net is that AP-alignment fixed the problems that were textbook-authored (word choice, local ordering) and left untouched the ones that are CED-architectural (Unit 3/Unit 4 split, no ΔG requirement, no scope for folding thermodynamics). Which is what you'd predict — a book aligned to a framework inherits that framework's structure and can only edit within it.

**For glycolysis specifically:** modest improvement. §7.2 now sits downstream of a §6.5 that has already introduced feedback and allosteric inhibition, so the regulatory vocabulary exists before the pathway does. The §7.7 consolidation is still late, and the free-energy grounding is still qualitative.
