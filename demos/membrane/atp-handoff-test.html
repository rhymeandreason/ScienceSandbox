mitochondrion — inner membrane The complex burns NADH to throw protons out; they come back through the synthase and it turns. The ATP is made in the matrix and leaves by two doors: a translocase, then a porin.
the cytosol
the cell surface — plasma membrane The Na⁺/K⁺ pump. It cannot turn without an ATP, so it waits for one. The cytosol under it is the strip on the left.

where the ATP goes

Two membranes, because they are two membranes. The synthase is in the inner mitochondrial one and the pump is in the plasma one, and the strip between them is the cytosol: the mitochondrion is sitting in it, the pump's lower face is on it, and the ATP is what crosses it. One ATP made, one turn of the pump bought: 3 Na⁺ out and 2 K⁺ in, both uphill.
