privacy

No ads, very minimal tracking.

Kodolab uses Vercel Web Analytics to count how many people visit and which lessons they open. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and does not identify you or follow you to any other site.

What is recorded for a page view: the page, the site that linked you here, your country, and your browser and device type. Your IP address is used to work out the country and is not stored. There is no advertising network, no third-party tracker, and nothing is sold or shared.

The AI features use Google's Gemini API. This includes the App Builder and any Question Box. We log questions and prompts that you type, anonymously, to improve the quality of responses and the overall experience.
