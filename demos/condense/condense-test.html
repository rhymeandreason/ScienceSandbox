condense-drag.js — test bench

Two mounts of one module. Drag the right-hand molecule onto the left-hand one. Nothing on this page decides any chemistry: which atoms react and which leave are read off each molecule's own condense: block, and the finished shape is the product spec's own coordinates.

A — two glucoses, and a fork. Drag the right ring so its anomeric oxygen meets the left ring's C4. The fork is the reagent, not the aim: β-glucose gives cellobiose (cellulose's repeat), α-glucose gives maltose (starch's). You cannot turn one into the other by rotating it — that oxygen is on the other side of the ring, and moving it means breaking a bond. Same water, different food.

B — two alanines, same module. The carboxyl keeps its carbon and loses –OH; the amino keeps its nitrogen and loses an H. One bond, one water, a peptide. No product spec exists for a dipeptide, so this join is posed as a schematic and claims no conformation — which is why this card says nothing about shape.
