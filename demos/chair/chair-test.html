chair-flip.js — test bench

Orbit it first: the ring is a chair, not a hexagon. Then flip it. Every substituent trades places between lying in the ring's plane (equatorial) and standing perpendicular to it (axial). Nothing below is typed — the counts and the colours come from the module measuring the geometry it just built.

equatorial — out in the ring's plane, out of everything's way axial — straight up or down, crowding whatever else is axial on the same face
