focus — look here

enzyme — the blob behind the substrate

Run the motion sequence with a blob on: it TRACKS, because it re-measures every frame. That is the whole design — a blob frozen at the moment its molecule landed keeps whatever else was still moving. Pin is the one exception, and it is the caller's call: it is how one enzyme holds a molecule that is about to become two (aldolase) or two that are about to become one (citrate synthase). Pin, then run the sequence: the blob must NOT follow.

Over BOTH is the case with no lesson coverage otherwise — one blob spanning two subjects, sized by the circle round all of them.
