Test — does the hexokinase domain closure read as a hinge?

The bench for the question the blob in glycolysis-lab currently ducks: at step 1 an enzyme is drawn as a featureless disc, because at that scale a drawn protein would be wrong about fold, size and active site alike. This asks whether a real one is worth drawing instead.

Green = measured. Both endpoints are deposited yeast hexokinase, and every number in the panel is read from the bake, not typed here.

The caveat, stated because it is real: the open form is isozyme PII and the closed form is PI. They are 77% identical, but the angle is closure plus whatever the two isozymes differ by, and those cannot be separated from deposited files. The same-isozyme candidates, 1HKG and 2YHX, have 83 and 78 residues the depositors could not identify, so no residue-level path exists along them.
