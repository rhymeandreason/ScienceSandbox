Prototype — collapsed-helix tube render.
One continuous tube per chain, wide through each helix and thin through each loop, with the radius ramped so the change is a taper rather than a step. Helices are collapsed onto their axis — no turn-by-turn corkscrew. Heme is a single sphere at the iron, no porphyrin ring. Four draw calls for the tetramer, on purpose: the target is animating several at once.
