All four entries are fluorescent and all four are engineered: every one carries Q80R, and none of them is the wild-type protein. What changes the colour is the made from row, which is read off the atoms rather than off the sequence records.

Every view is superposed on 1EMA by its α-carbons, so flipping between them moves only what actually moved. All five are the same protein under the same numbering.

Ribbon colour is secondary structure, the repo default. The ball-and-stick inside is the chromophore and the five side chains around it — Arg96 and Glu222 below, His148, Thr203 and Ser205 on the phenol end. Chain A's, on every view.
