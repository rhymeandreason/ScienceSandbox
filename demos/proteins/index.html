Every structure the repo holds, and what it is for. Each card opens on the variant that protein is indexed by; the bench behind it is where the rest were reviewed.

Names, blurbs and purposes are written by hand; methods, chain and residue counts are written by the bakers and rewritten on every re-bake, so nothing on a card can drift from the file it describes. proteins/proteins.js is the registry, and proteins/check-proteins.js is what fails a commit where the two disagree.
