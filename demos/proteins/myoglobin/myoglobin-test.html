Every view is superposed on deoxy (1BZP) by its heme, matched atom by atom, so flipping between states moves only what actually moved. Seven crystals otherwise means seven arbitrary orientations, and the molecule jumps on every click.

Ribbon colour is secondary structure, the repo default. The pocket is drawn ball-and-stick: the heme with its iron in rust, whatever is bound to it, and the two histidines — proximal below the ring, distal above it — with their backbone left to the ribbon.

Turn the molecule, then copy. Myoglobin is a bundle whose three axes are too close together for a solver to choose between, so the orientation is a human's — and because all seven views share one frame, one choice re-aims every one of them. Paste it into view.basis in proteins.js with by: 'human', then re-run the baker: the registry is the only place a chosen basis is written, and the baker is what puts it in the file every page reads.
