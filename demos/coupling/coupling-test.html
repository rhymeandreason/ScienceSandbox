coupling.js — test bench

Three mounts of the same module. Nothing on this page styles it, and every ΔG below is a published standard value carried on the scenario — none is typed into the figure.

A — the sum is what decides. Hexokinase, the worked example every textbook runs. Drag the slider: somewhere past +30.5 the total crosses zero and the verdict flips, because ATP can only pay for what it can cover.

B — take the link away. The one to spend time on. Untick the shared phosphate and nothing else changes: same two reactions, same two ΔG, same beaker. The uphill one stops running, because ATP was never giving energy to glucose — it was handing over a phosphate.

C — the same arithmetic, spending a drop to bank an ATP. Glycolysis step 10. PEP falls a long way; making ATP costs +30.5 of it, and the cell keeps the difference. Coupling is not a thing that only rescues uphill reactions.
