reaction/ — every verb, on a molecule no pathway names

The module claims its verbs are chemistry and the lanes are only a stage fact. Nothing ran that claim outside glycolysis, the cycle and fermentation until this page. Two passes, and the second is the one that prices the next caller.

1 · Every verb runs on a probe

Each verb is driven on a probe built here from SkelLib, carrying only the metadata that verb reads and nothing else. A probe is not a molecule and makes no chemical claim — it is a carbon chain with one feature bolted on, so nothing here is a statement about a reaction. What it asserts is about the code: a verb that needs a glycolysis intermediate to run has chemistry the module was not supposed to own, and it fails visibly in this column.

“did nothing” is the result that matters. Nine of these verbs return early on a missing metadata key with no error at all, so a bench that only caught throws would call them green. Motion, fx and leaving are counted through each run; zero of all three is a verb that was invoked and did not happen.

2 · What a verb needs beyond the stage

The same verbs, against a host that is Reaction.stageHost() plus specOf and meta and nothing else: no carrierPoint, no carrierBond, no onCarrierTaken, no donorSpec, freeSpec, waterSpec or handleSpec. A verb that survives is one a lesson with no carrier tray can mount today. A verb that throws names the member it wanted, and that list is the cost of the next non-pathway caller — beta-oxidation, the Calvin cycle — measured instead of discovered halfway through building one.

Pass 1's last column says what a verb used; this says what it cannot do without. A member in one and not the other is a guarded optional, which is the module behaving.

3 · The same verbs, on a lesson

Two glucoses become a disaccharide and a water leaves; then a water arrives and the bond comes apart. Real specs out of the library, whose condense and glycosidic blocks already name every atom that moves — nothing is authored here and nothing is typed. The product, the linkage and the configuration are read off the molecules, so the caption cannot claim a linkage the spec does not have.

β-glucose reaches cellobiose and α-glucose reaches maltose: two molecules, one difference, and it is the difference between wood and bread. Which verbs the module supplied and which the page had to write is the last column, and it is the answer to whether any of this is reusable.
