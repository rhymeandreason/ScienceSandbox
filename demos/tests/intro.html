Why

Seeing it beats reading about it.

Students spend their attention reconciling static pictures instead of learning the concept. These pages pull the scattered versions of an idea into one model you can turn, poke, and break.

Kodolab is a library of in-depth 3D interactives for threshold concepts, the ideas a student has to cross before the rest of biology makes sense.
