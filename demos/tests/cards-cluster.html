live 3D on a card, on a budget of four live 0 / 4 · click a card to run it

Three kinds of card on one budget: six of water/watersim.js, two bonding builders with an orthographic camera of their own, and one kit/molbox.js close-up, which runs no simulation at all. Four can be live at once; opening a fifth releases whichever has gone longest without being opened, and that card keeps the last frame it drew. Scroll a card off screen and its loop stops on its own.

What this bench is testing

  • The context budget. Browsers cap WebGL contexts near 8-16 and drop the oldest with no error. CardStage.pool rations them, so a map with twenty cards never makes twenty stages.
  • Pausing keeps the picture. stop() leaves the last frame on the canvas, and an evicted card pins a snapshot of it — losing the stage never means losing the picture.
  • Off screen costs nothing. An IntersectionObserver stops the loop without the page asking.
  • WaterSim still owns no stage. It takes a root Object3D, and this page hands it one per card.
  • Two kinds of card, one budget. The builder card is orthographic and the water cards are not, because that card compares atom sizes and perspective would make a nearer atom read bigger. Finish the molecule and it turns itself after 900 ms — the beat that says the flat cross was a drawing, not a shape.
